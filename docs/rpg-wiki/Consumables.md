# Consumables

Potions restore HP instantly (see [Combat System](Combat%20System.md) for how HP persists between fights).

- **🧪 Minor Potion** — 400 gold — restores 25% of max HP
- **⚗️ Greater Potion** — 4,000 gold — restores 55% of max HP
- **💠 Superior Potion** — 18,000 gold — restores 100% of max HP

Buy with `/rpgbuy`, use with `/rpguse`, sell unwanted potions with `/rpgsell`. See [Commands](Commands.md).
