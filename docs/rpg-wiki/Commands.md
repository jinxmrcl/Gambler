# Commands

## Character
- `/rpgstart` — create your character (pick a [[RPG Overview#Classes|class]])
- `/character` — view your sheet
- `/classes` — list all classes
- `/heal` — pay gold to restore HP / revive

## Dungeons
- `/dungeons` — list all [[RPG Overview#Dungeons (in level order)|dungeons]]
- `/dungeon` — fight a regular monster
- `/dungeonboss` — challenge a dungeon's [[RPG Overview#Bosses|boss]]

## Shop
- `/rpgshop` — browse [[Equipment & Upgrades|gear]] and [[Consumables|potions]]
- `/rpgbuy`, `/rpgequip`, `/rpguse`, `/rpgsell`, `/rpgupgrade`, `/rpginventory`

## PvP
- `/duel` — challenge another player (always full HP, see [[Combat System]])
- `/arena` — leaderboard
