# Economy Overview

The core currency loop — every player has a wallet balance and a bank balance (bank money is
safe from `/rob`).

- [Payday](Payday.md) — fully passive, no command — random amount at a random personal interval
- [Bank](Bank.md) — depositing/withdrawing to protect money from robbery
- [Hustle (Work Crime Rob)](Hustle%20%28Work%20Crime%20Rob%29.md) — risk-based income commands
- [Shop & Inventory](Shop%20%26%20Inventory.md) — the rob shield and cooldown-reset items
