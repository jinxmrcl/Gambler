# Balance & Daily

- `/balance` — view your (or another player's) wallet balance
- `/daily` — claim a once-per-24h bonus, claimed atomically in the database to close a
  double-claim race from firing the command twice at once
- `/leaderboard` — richest players, most games played, biggest spenders, biggest wins, or most
  successful robberies
- `/pay` — transfer balance to another player, atomic single-transaction transfer
