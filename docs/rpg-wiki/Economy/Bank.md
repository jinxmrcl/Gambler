# Bank

- `/bank` — shows cash and bank balance
- `/deposit` / `/withdraw` — move money between wallet and bank (accepts a number, `half`, or `all`)

Money in the bank can't be stolen by [`/rob`](Hustle%20%28Work%20Crime%20Rob%29.md). Deposits/withdrawals
run as atomic transactions. See [Economy Overview](Economy%20Overview.md).
