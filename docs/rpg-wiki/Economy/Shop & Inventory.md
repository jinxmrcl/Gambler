# Shop & Inventory

Two purchasable items (`utils/items.py`):

- **🛡️ Rob Shield** — 750 gold — protects from `/rob` for
  2 hours
- **⏩ Cooldown Reset** — 1000 gold — instantly
  resets [[Hustle (Work Crime Rob)|work/crime/slut/rob]] (and dungeon/duel) cooldowns

Commands: `/shop`, `/buy`, `/inventory`, `/use`, `/gift`. See [[Economy Overview]].
