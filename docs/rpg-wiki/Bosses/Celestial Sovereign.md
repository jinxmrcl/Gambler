# Celestial Sovereign 🌌

Boss of [[Dungeons/Celestial Rift]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 2391 • ATK 136 • DEF 66 • Crit 20%
- Rewards: 3760 XP, 5000-7600 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
