# Leviathan King 🐋

Boss of [[Dungeons/Sunken Ruins]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 3523 • ATK 209 • DEF 94 • Crit 16%
- Rewards: 4880 XP, 6080-9226 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
