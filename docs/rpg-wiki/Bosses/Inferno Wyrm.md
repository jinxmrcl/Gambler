# Inferno Wyrm 🌋

Boss of [Molten Depths](../Dungeons/Molten%20Depths.md). Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 1050 • ATK 58 • DEF 28 • Crit 17%
- Rewards: 1333 XP, 1760-2733 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md).
