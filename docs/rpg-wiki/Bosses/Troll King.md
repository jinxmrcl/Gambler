# Troll King 👑

Boss of [[Dungeons/Shadowed Cave]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 396 • ATK 28 • DEF 9 • Crit 15%
- Rewards: 286 XP, 400-706 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
