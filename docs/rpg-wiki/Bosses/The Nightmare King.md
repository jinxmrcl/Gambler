# The Nightmare King 👹

Boss of [Nightmare Realm](../Dungeons/Nightmare%20Realm.md). Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 9216 • ATK 558 • DEF 254 • Crit 20%
- Rewards: 13013 XP, 16080-24266 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md).
