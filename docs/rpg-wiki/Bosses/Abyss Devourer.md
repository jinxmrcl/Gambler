# Abyss Devourer 👁️

Boss of [[Dungeons/Endless Abyss]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 1470 • ATK 89 • DEF 40 • Crit 20%
- Rewards: 2160 XP, 2893-4400 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
