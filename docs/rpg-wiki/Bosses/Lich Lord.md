# Lich Lord 💀

Boss of [[Dungeons/Forgotten Crypt]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 676 • ATK 44 • DEF 18 • Crit 16%
- Rewards: 680 XP, 853-1360 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
