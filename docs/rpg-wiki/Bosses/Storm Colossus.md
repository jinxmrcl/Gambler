# Storm Colossus 🌪️

Boss of [[Dungeons/Scorched Wastes]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 6953 • ATK 418 • DEF 189 • Crit 19%
- Rewards: 9733 XP, 12080-18266 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
