# Elder Treant 🌳

Boss of [[Dungeons/Whispering Forest]]. Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 157 • ATK 16 • DEF 3 • Crit 13%
- Rewards: 120 XP, 146-280 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [[Combat System]].
