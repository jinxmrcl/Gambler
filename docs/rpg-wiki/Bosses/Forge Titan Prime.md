# Forge Titan Prime ⚒️

Boss of [Titan's Forge](../Dungeons/Titan%27s%20Forge.md). Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 25386 • ATK 1530 • DEF 709 • Crit 20%
- Rewards: 35653 XP, 44080-66320 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md).
