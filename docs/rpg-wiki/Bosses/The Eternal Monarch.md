# The Eternal Monarch 🏰

Boss of [Eternal Throne](../Dungeons/Eternal%20Throne.md). Challenge with `/dungeonboss` (5 minute cooldown per dungeon).

## Stats (base, before level scaling)
- HP 41475 • ATK 2504 • DEF 1160 • Crit 21%
- Rewards: 58400 XP, 72080-108320 gold

Boss stats are 3.5x HP / 1.6x ATK+DEF / 4x XP+gold over the dungeon's average regular monster — see [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md).
