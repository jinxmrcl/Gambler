# Primordial Tier ✨

A 7th tier above Ancient. Drop-only — never appears in `/rpgshop`, can't be bought,
sold, or enchanted through the normal system.

## Base items

- **✨ Primordial Edge** (weapon) — +70% ATK
- **✨ Primordial Aegis** (armor) — +49% DEF, +70% HP
- **✨ Primordial Relic** (accessory) — +28% Crit

## Affixes

Every drop rolls 1-3 random bonus affixes (weighted so 3 is rare) from:

| Affix | Range |
|---|---|
| ATK | +5% to +15% |
| DEF | +5% to +15% |
| HP | +5% to +15% |
| Crit Chance | +3% to +10% |
| Lifesteal | +5% to +15% (heals you on *every* hit, not just once) |
| Crit Damage | +10% to +40% (pushes crits above the flat 2.0x multiplier) |
| Damage Reflect | +5% to +15% (reflects a slice of damage taken back at the attacker) |
| Gold Find | +10% to +30% (bonus gold from every source during a run) |

Equipping a Primordial item in a slot fully replaces your regular gear in that slot
(and its enchant level) for as long as it's equipped — see
`/rpgequipprimordial`/`/rpgunequipprimordial` in [Commands](../Commands.md).

## Dropping

Only from a boss kill in one of the 5 high-end dungeons — [Titan's Forge](../Dungeons/Titan%27s%20Forge.md),
[Chaos Rift](../Dungeons/Chaos%20Rift.md), [Eternal Throne](../Dungeons/Eternal%20Throne.md),
[World Ender's Lair](../Dungeons/World%20Ender%27s%20Lair.md), and [Voidscar Expanse](../Dungeons/Voidscar%20Expanse.md)
— at a small independent 4% chance, separate from normal loot. In a
[team boss fight](../Combat%20System.md#team-fights) it rolls once for the whole party
rather than per member, and goes to one random surviving party member.

See [Equipment & Upgrades](../Equipment%20%26%20Upgrades.md) for the rest of the gear system.
