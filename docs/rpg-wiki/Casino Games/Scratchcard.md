# Scratchcard 🎫

Click each tile of a 3x3 grid yourself to scratch it off — the whole card is decided the moment
you play, clicking just reveals it. Match 4 or more of the same symbol to win; the highest-paying
symbol among your matches decides the payout (🍀 0.67x, 🎁 1x, 🔔 1.67x, 💰 3.34x, 💎 8.36x, 👑
20.06x). Times out after 60s, auto-revealing the rest. See [Casino Overview](Casino%20Overview.md).
