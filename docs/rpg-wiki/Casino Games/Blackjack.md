# Blackjack 🃏

Classic Blackjack with Hit / Stand / Double buttons, interactive via Discord Components V2.

- Natural blackjack (2-card 21) pays **2.5x**
- Regular win pays **2x**, push refunds the bet
- Double down doubles the bet and draws exactly one more card
- Dealer draws to 17
- While the dealer's second card is still face-down, their visible card's value is shown
  as text (e.g. "**7** showing") alongside the card emoji, so it's readable even if the
  emoji itself renders small or blurry on your client

Part of [Casino Overview](Casino%20Overview.md). Uses the shared 3% edge implicitly through payout ratios.
