# Horse Race 🏇

Bet first, then pick your horse from a dropdown of 6 randomly-named runners, and watch an
animated race play out. Each horse has a different speed profile, so odds aren't even — they're
derived by actually simulating 60,000 races ahead of time and paying `house_edge_adjusted_RTP /
win_probability` per horse, not hand-picked. That currently lands around 2.7x-3.1x for the two
favorites up to ~40x for the two longshots. Don't pick in time and the bet is refunded. See
[[Casino Overview]].
