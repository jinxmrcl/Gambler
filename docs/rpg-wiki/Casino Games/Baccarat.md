# Baccarat 🎴

Bet on Player, Banker, or Tie. Hands are dealt and drawn using the real casino rulebook —
natural 8/9 stands immediately, otherwise Player draws a third card at 0-5 and Banker's third
card follows the standard banker-draw table reacting to Player's third card — then plays out
with an animated reveal. Odds are calibrated by simulating 80,000 hands rather than assumed:
currently ~1.97x on Player, ~1.9x on Banker, ~10.34x on Tie. A Tie push refunds Player/Banker
bets instead of losing them. See [Casino Overview](Casino%20Overview.md).
