# Marriage

- `/marry` — propose to another player (Accept/Decline buttons)
- `/divorce` — ends the marriage
- `/marriage` — check marriage status

## Marriage Bank
A shared pool either spouse can deposit into or withdraw from — money only becomes "theirs to
grab" once someone actually deposits it, unlike directly draining a partner's personal wallet.

- `/spousebank`, `/spousedeposit`, `/spousewithdraw`
- On divorce, any remaining pool balance is split 50/50 between both partners.
