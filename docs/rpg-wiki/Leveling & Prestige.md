# Leveling & Prestige

Levels run from 1 to 1500. Every 50 levels is one Prestige tier (Prestige 1 through 29), each with its own badge shown on your [character sheet](Commands.md#character).

Level 51 displays as **Prestige 1, Level 1**; level 1500 (max) displays as **Prestige 29, Level 50**.

Prestige isn't purely cosmetic — every tier grants a small permanent +0.5% multiplier to HP/ATK/DEF, applied on top of class stats, equipment, and Primordial affixes. That tops out at **+14.5%** at Prestige 29 (max level).

## Titles
Your title is based on total level, independent of prestige display:

- **Novice** — level 1+
- **Adventurer** — level 5+
- **Veteran** — level 15+
- **Champion** — level 25+
- **Legend** — level 40+
- **Ascended** — level 51+
- **Mythic** — level 101+
- **Immortal** — level 251+
- **Godlike** — level 501+
- **Transcendent** — level 751+
- **Eternal** — level 1001+

XP needed for a level: `45 * level^1.42` — a gentler curve than the original `50 * level^1.5`, roughly halving total XP needed to reach level 1500. See [Combat System](Combat%20System.md) for how XP is earned (dungeon and boss kills, duel wins).
