# Druid 🌿

A nature warden who sustains through the fight with continuous regeneration.

## Base stats (level 1)
- HP: 95 (+9.88/level)
- ATK: 15 (+2.47/level)
- DEF: 5 (+1.48/level)
- Crit: 8%

## Skill
**Regrowth** — After every exchange, recovers 4% of missing HP.

Unlike the heal built into [Paladin](Paladin.md)'s Divine Shield (a single threshold-triggered heal), Regrowth has no once-per-fight limit or HP threshold — it ticks continuously for the whole fight.

See [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md) for how skills interact with the damage formula, and [Equipment & Upgrades](../Equipment%20%26%20Upgrades.md) for gearing this class.
