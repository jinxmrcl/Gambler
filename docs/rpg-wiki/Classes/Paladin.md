# Paladin ✨

A balanced holy warrior with steady sustain.

## Base stats (level 1)
- HP: 105 (+10.5/level)
- ATK: 13 (+1.91/level)
- DEF: 8 (+1.91/level)
- Crit: 8%

## Skill
**Divine Shield** — Starts every fight with a shield absorbing 20% of max HP, and heals 15% of max HP the first time you drop below 30%.

The shield is a flat damage-absorption buffer that soaks incoming damage before it touches real HP — it depletes as it absorbs hits and doesn't regenerate mid-fight, but stacks with the Lay on Hands-style heal for genuine front-loaded and reactive sustain together.

See [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md) for how skills interact with the damage formula, and [Equipment & Upgrades](../Equipment%20%26%20Upgrades.md) for gearing this class.
