# Paladin ✨

A balanced holy warrior with steady sustain.

## Base stats (level 1)
- HP: 105 (+10.5/level)
- ATK: 13 (+1.91/level)
- DEF: 8 (+1.91/level)
- Crit: 8%

## Skill
**Divine Shield** — Starts every fight with a shield absorbing 20% of max HP, and heals 15% of max HP the first time you drop below 30%.

The shield is a flat damage-absorption buffer that soaks incoming damage before it touches real HP — it depletes as it absorbs hits and doesn't regenerate mid-fight, but stacks with the Lay on Hands-style heal for genuine front-loaded and reactive sustain together.

## Shields
Paladin is the only class that can equip a shield — a 4th gear slot on top of weapon/armor/accessory, across the same 6 tiers (`/rpgshop`, `/rpgequip`). Every shield grants both flat damage reduction and a chance to fully block a hit outright, on top of Divine Shield's own HP-absorption buffer — a Paladin fully geared for it becomes genuinely difficult to bring down.
