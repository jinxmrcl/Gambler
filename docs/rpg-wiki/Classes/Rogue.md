# Rogue 🗡️

A quick striker who lands brutal critical hits.

## Base stats (level 1)
- HP: 95 (+12.09/level)
- ATK: 18 (+2.2/level)
- DEF: 7 (+1.1/level)
- Crit: 27%

## Skill
**Backstab** — The first attack of every fight deals 70% bonus damage.

See [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md) for how skills interact with the damage formula, and [Equipment & Upgrades](../Equipment%20%26%20Upgrades.md) for gearing this class.
