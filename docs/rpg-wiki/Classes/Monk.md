# Monk 🥋

A disciplined martial artist who punishes attackers for every strike landed.

## Base stats (level 1)
- HP: 100 (+9.94/level)
- ATK: 16 (+2.49/level)
- DEF: 8 (+1.49/level)
- Crit: 10%

## Skill
**Counterstrike** — Every time you're hit, a 25% chance to immediately strike back for 50% damage.

Unlike the other classes' skills, Counterstrike isn't a one-time first-hit effect — it can trigger on any hit you take, all fight long.

See [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md) for how skills interact with the damage formula, and [Equipment & Upgrades](../Equipment%20%26%20Upgrades.md) for gearing this class.
