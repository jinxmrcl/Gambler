# Necromancer 💀

A dark caster who drains life from their enemies to sustain themselves.

## Base stats (level 1)
- HP: 90 (+8.56/level)
- ATK: 21 (+2.85/level)
- DEF: 6 (+1.9/level)
- Crit: 9%

## Skill
**Life Drain** — The first attack of every fight heals you for two-thirds of the damage dealt.

See [Combat System](../Combat%20System.md) for how skills interact with the damage formula, and [Equipment & Upgrades](../Equipment%20%26%20Upgrades.md) for gearing this class.
