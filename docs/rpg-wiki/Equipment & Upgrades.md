# Equipment & Upgrades

Three gear slots: weapon (+ATK), armor (+DEF, +HP), and accessory (+Crit chance). Bonuses are percentage-based so gear stays relevant all the way to level 1500.

## Tiers

- ⚪ [[Equipment Tiers/Common Tier]] — 200 gold base price
- 🔵 [[Equipment Tiers/Rare Tier]] — 800 gold base price
- 🟣 [[Equipment Tiers/Epic Tier]] — 2,500 gold base price
- 🟠 [[Equipment Tiers/Legendary Tier]] — 7,000 gold base price
- 🔴 [[Equipment Tiers/Mythic Tier]] — 20,000 gold base price
- 🌈 [[Equipment Tiers/Ancient Tier]] — 60,000 gold base price

## Upgrading (enchanting)
Spend gold via `/rpgupgrade` to enchant your *equipped* item in a slot, up to +10. Each level adds another 1% on top of that item's own bonus. Cost scales with the item's tier and current enchant level.

## Selling
Any owned item (gear or a [[Consumables|potion]]) can be sold back with `/rpgsell` for 40% of its shop price.
